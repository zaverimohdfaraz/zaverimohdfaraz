**Tech:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `MongoDB` `Nodemailer` `Vercel`

### 🔹 MK Marine Services — Website & Operations Portal

A business platform designed for a marine consulting and vessel spare-parts trading company.

The project combines a public-facing business website with an internal operations portal for managing clients, vessels, enquiries, products, quotations, sales, purchases, payments, suppliers, reports and business tasks.

The portal is designed around centralized coordination between team members working from different locations.

**Tech:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `React`
